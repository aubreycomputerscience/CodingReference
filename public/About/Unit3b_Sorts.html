Encapsulation: Group related data and operations behind a defined interface. Object-oriented design: Organize a program around objects that combine state and behavior and collaborate to accomplish a task. Abstract data type: Specify a type by its operations and behavior independently of its implementation. A class can represent: - A real-world thing, such as a car or bank account. - A conceptual entity, such as a playlist or shopping cart. - A software responsibility, such as a sort manager or authentication service. All three can be legitimate object-oriented designs.
