Mr. C

Mr. Cline has worked as a software architect and iOS developer in Silicon Valley, taught iOS development through Facebook University to students from Stanford, Princeton, and Yale, and written numerous books and articles about the impact of emerging technologies. His undergraduate degrees are in Engineering Physics and Philosophy, and his graduate degree focused on Human-Computer Interaction and the social and historical impact of communication technologies.

Mr. Cline’s hobbies include surfing, travel, philosophy, and writing. He has traveled to 37 countries and lived in or had extended homestays in China, Russia, India, Japan, Singapore, Vietnam, Germany, and Mexico. He has previously taught courses including AP Calculus, Theory of Knowledge, and Computers, Culture, and Society.
