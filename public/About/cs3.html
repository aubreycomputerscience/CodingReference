COMPUTER SCIENCE III

Computer Science III moves from building applications to understanding and implementing the deeper structures and systems that software is built on. Students work with advanced data structures and algorithms, including linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, heaps, graphs, sets, maps, hashing, quicksort, and heapsort. The course also includes object-oriented design, UML, recursion, testing with assertions and invariants, error handling, low-level programming such as assembly, operating-system scripting, secure programming, and emerging technologies.
