Welcome to Mr. C's Computer Science

Our goal is to teach students to build real-world apps using real tools and to develop real-world skills. Rather than simply working through a textbook, students will learn computer science by building lightweight apps and games. App development is the entry point into learning computer science, not the other way around.

In this process, students will learn programming fundamentals, development tools, data structures, and other core concepts. We will not use reactive frameworks. Instead, the course emphasizes programming fundamentals through an imperative, event-driven, MVC approach. Students will also learn how various technologies work and discuss the social and ethical impact of computer technologies.

Learning to code can be difficult because there is a lot of friction. Setting up a development environment can be complicated and difficult to understand. Reference materials are often written for professional developers, and the first time you Google a programming question, it can feel like stepping into a vast, incomprehensible universe. Sometimes your code will not compile, and you will have no idea why.

Through carefully constructed and curated materials, we have eliminated much of this friction from this class. However, becoming a professional developer still requires patience and grit. Programming requires a lifelong commitment to learning, solving complex problems, and overcoming adversity.
