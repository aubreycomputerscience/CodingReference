Careers in Hardware, Networks, and IT Operations

Modern organizations depend on computers, networks, servers, cloud services, and other infrastructure. IT professionals install, configure, maintain, troubleshoot, and protect these systems so that other people can do their jobs.

Example Careers

  • IT Support Specialist — helps users solve problems with computers, software, and other technology.
  • Network Engineer — builds and maintains the networks that connect computers and devices.
  • Systems Administrator — manages the computers and servers used by an organization.
  • Cloud Engineer — builds and manages systems that run using cloud services.
  • Cybersecurity Analyst — looks for security problems and helps protect systems and data.

Some of these jobs focus on helping individual users, while others involve managing technology used by thousands or even millions of people.
