Careers in Business Technology

Many technology professionals do not build software from scratch. Instead, they help organizations choose, customize, connect, and automate existing technology to solve business problems.

Example Careers

  • Business Systems Analyst — studies how an organization works and helps determine what technology it needs.
  • Technology Consultant — helps organizations use technology to solve business problems.
  • Salesforce Developer — customizes Salesforce software to meet an organization's needs.
  • Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Consultant — helps companies implement systems that manage areas such as finance, inventory, and operations.
  • Agentic Workflow Engineer — uses AI agents and automation to improve how work is performed.

These careers often combine technical knowledge with an understanding of how businesses and organizations actually operate.
