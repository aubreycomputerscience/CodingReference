Careers in Technology Leadership

Large technology projects require people who can organize work, communicate with different groups, and help teams decide what to build. These careers sit between technology, business, and management.

Example Careers

  • IT Project Manager — organizes projects, schedules work, and helps teams complete them successfully.
  • Product Manager — helps decide what a technology product should do and which features should be built.
  • Business Analyst — helps translate business needs into requirements for a technology project.
  • Technology Manager — leads technical teams and helps coordinate their work.

During your final project, you will practice some of these same skills by planning work, dividing responsibilities, making design decisions, and managing a project from an idea to a finished product.
