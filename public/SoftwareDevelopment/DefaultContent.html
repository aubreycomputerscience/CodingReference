How to Use This Website

This website is a coding reference organized by topic. It is not designed to be read from beginning to end or worked through step by step. Instead, lessons will be assigned throughout the course as they become relevant.

The material is divided into chapters. Introductory lessons generally appear near the beginning of each chapter, while foundational topics tend to appear in the earlier chapters. You can also return to these lessons whenever you need a reminder or want to look something up.
