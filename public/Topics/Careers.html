Which Information Technology Career Fits You?

Not everyone in technology does the same kind of work. Some people build new things. Others keep technology running. Some help businesses solve problems, analyze information, lead teams, or develop the technologies of the future.

As you read, check the statements that sound most like you. You may check more than one. Most technology careers combine several different kinds of work.

☐ I enjoy creating things.

```

You like designing, inventing, and bringing ideas to life.

You might enjoy:

  • Software Developer — builds computer programs and applications.
  • Mobile App Developer — creates apps for phones and tablets.
  • Web Developer — builds websites and web applications.
  • Game Developer — creates the software behind computer and video games.

Typical work:

  • Design new apps, websites, and software
  • Write code
  • Solve programming problems
  • Work with designers, customers, and other developers
  • Build products people use
```

☐ I enjoy figuring out how things work.

```

You like troubleshooting, fixing problems, and making systems reliable.

You might enjoy:

  • Network Engineer — builds and maintains the networks that connect computers and devices.
  • Systems Administrator — keeps an organization's computers and servers running.
  • Cloud Engineer — builds and manages computer systems that run on cloud services.
  • Cybersecurity Analyst — looks for security problems and helps protect systems from attack.
  • AI Safety / Security Engineer — tests AI systems for problems and helps make deployed AI safer and more reliable.
  • IT Support Specialist — helps people solve problems with computers, software, and other technology.

Typical work:

  • Keep computer systems running
  • Diagnose and solve technical problems
  • Protect systems and data from attacks
  • Test systems for weaknesses and failures
  • Configure networks, computers, and cloud systems
  • Help people use technology successfully
```

☐ I enjoy solving business problems.

```

You like understanding how organizations work and using technology to improve them.

You might enjoy:

  • Business Systems Analyst — studies how a business works and helps determine what technology it needs.
  • Technology Consultant — helps organizations choose and use technology to solve problems.
  • Salesforce Developer — customizes Salesforce software to fit the needs of a business.
  • Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Consultant — helps companies set up software that manages areas such as finance, inventory, and operations.
  • Agentic Workflow Engineer — uses AI agents to automate tasks and improve how work gets done.

Typical work:

  • Understand how a business works
  • Meet with customers and employees
  • Improve company workflows
  • Configure and customize business software
  • Automate repetitive or complex work
  • Write code or connect existing technologies when needed
```

☐ I enjoy working with data.

```

You like finding patterns, answering questions, and using information to understand what is happening.

You might enjoy:

  • Data Analyst — examines data to answer questions and find useful patterns.
  • Data Scientist — uses data, statistics, and computing to solve complex problems and make predictions.
  • AI / Machine Learning Engineer — builds and improves systems that learn from data.
  • Database Administrator — manages the databases organizations use to store important information.
  • Data Engineer — builds systems that collect, organize, and move large amounts of data.
  • Business Intelligence Analyst — turns business data into reports and information that help people make decisions.

Typical work:

  • Organize and analyze data
  • Find patterns and trends
  • Create reports and visualizations
  • Train and evaluate machine-learning models
  • Build systems for storing and processing data
  • Help organizations make decisions using evidence
```

☐ I enjoy organizing people and ideas.

```

You like planning, communicating, making decisions, and helping groups work toward a common goal.

You might enjoy:

  • IT Project Manager — organizes technology projects and helps teams finish them successfully.
  • Product Manager — helps decide what a technology product should do and what the team should build next.
  • Business Analyst — helps translate business problems and needs into requirements for a project.
  • Technology Manager — leads technology teams and helps organize their work.

Typical work:

  • Plan projects
  • Coordinate teams
  • Meet with customers and stakeholders
  • Translate business needs into technical work
  • Help decide what should be built and when
```

☐ I enjoy designing how technology fits together.

```

You like looking at the big picture and deciding how different technologies can work together to solve a problem.

You might enjoy:

  • Solutions Architect — designs a technical solution to meet a customer's or organization's needs.
  • Software Architect — designs the overall structure of large software applications.
  • Cloud Architect — designs computer systems that run using cloud services.
  • Systems Engineer — designs and connects the different parts of a complex technology system.

Typical work:

  • Design large technology systems
  • Decide how different components work together
  • Compare possible technical solutions
  • Work with developers, managers, and customers
  • Make important technical design decisions
```

☐ I enjoy research and designing new technology.

```

You like asking questions that do not have easy answers, experimenting with new ideas, and figuring out how future technologies might work.

You might enjoy:

  • AI / Foundation Model Researcher — develops new methods for building and improving advanced AI models.
  • AI Safety Researcher — studies how advanced AI systems behave, what can go wrong, and how they can be made safer.
  • Computer Hardware / Chip Designer — designs the processors and other hardware that make computers work.
  • Robotics Engineer / Researcher — develops machines that can sense, move, and act in the physical world.

Typical work:

  • Explore problems that may not yet have known solutions
  • Design experiments and test new ideas
  • Develop new algorithms, models, hardware, or systems
  • Analyze results and improve designs
  • Work with engineers and other researchers
  • Help create technologies that may be used in future products
```

Technology Careers Are Not Just for Computer Science People

Different Backgrounds on a Software Team

Software development teams include more than programmers.

  • Artists and designers may work in user experience (UX), user interface design, graphic design, animation, or game art.
  • Anthropologists, psychologists, and other social scientists may work as user researchers, studying how people use technology and helping teams design better products.
  • Business professionals may work as product managers or project managers, helping decide what to build and organizing the work required to build it.

Other Careers Connected to Technology

Technology also intersects with many other professions. Technical writers explain complex technology, while lawyers and policy specialists may work on issues such as privacy, intellectual property, cybersecurity, and technology regulation.

People in almost any field—from medicine and finance to education, science, manufacturing, and entertainment—can specialize in the technology used in that field.

Remember

Technology careers are about much more than programming.

Every school, hospital, bank, airline, retailer, manufacturer, and government agency depends on people who understand and manage technology.

You also do not have to fit neatly into one category. A software developer might love design. A cybersecurity professional might enjoy investigation and communication. A product manager might understand both technology and business.

The best career fit is often the one that matches the kinds of problems you enjoy solving and the kind of work you enjoy doing.
