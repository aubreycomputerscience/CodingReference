Money Matters Rubric

1. Understanding & Reflection

  • Responds thoughtfully to warm-ups and teasers
  • Connects ideas to personal finance concepts
  • Explains reasoning, opinions, or choices
  • Shows understanding of the topic
  • Uses examples or evidence when appropriate
  • Engages seriously with questions even when there is no single correct answer

TEKS Alignment: Technical / Occupational Skills; Problem Solving / Critical Thinking; Professional, Ethical & Legal Responsibilities

2. Work Quality & Completion

  • Completes assigned work
  • Follows directions and requirements
  • Provides sufficient detail
  • Work is accurate when accuracy is relevant
  • Work is organized and understandable
  • Produces work appropriate to the assignment

TEKS Alignment: Technical / Occupational Skills; Technology / Tools / Processes; Professional Standards / Employability Skills

3. Communication & Participation

  • Participates appropriately in class discussions
  • Communicates ideas clearly
  • Listens and responds to others
  • Asks relevant questions
  • Contributes constructively to activities
  • Uses appropriate financial vocabulary when relevant

TEKS Alignment: Communication; Collaboration / Teamwork / Leadership; Problem Solving / Critical Thinking

4. Professionalism & Behavior

  • Uses class time productively
  • Comes prepared and ready to participate
  • Meets classroom expectations
  • Uses technology and resources appropriately
  • Treats others respectfully
  • Takes responsibility for behavior and work

TEKS Alignment: Professional Standards / Employability Skills; Professional, Ethical & Legal Responsibilities; Safety / Rules / Procedures; Collaboration / Teamwork / Leadership
